TL;DR

I’m Ixhen, an iOS Tech Lead based in Munich, Germany. Since 2020, almost all of my work has lived in the automotive and connected-car space (Porsche, VW, Audi, Lamborghini): CarPlay, app architecture, and getting proofs of concept into production.

Lately I’m going deep on on-device AI: measuring what Core ML models actually cost on an iPhone in latency, memory, thermals and accuracy, in the open-source on-device-bench.

Yeah, I know my name sounds like a new iPhone model. Fitting, because since discovering my love for Swift in 2020, I haven’t really spent a day away from it. That’s a glitch in the matrix, if ever there was one.

It might sound cliché, but I really love software engineering. It still feels unreal somehow getting paid to do this.
That thrill still remains novel every time. The problem starts messy, and then I get to eliminate the white noise until the solution looks obvious (chef's kiss 🤌).
